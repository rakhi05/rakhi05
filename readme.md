## 🌌 My Story

Technology has never been just about writing code for me.

My journey began with learning programming fundamentals and building
simple web pages out of curiosity. Every new concept pushed me to
explore a little further---from C to Java, from front-end development to
Python, and eventually into the fascinating world of Artificial
Intelligence and Machine Learning.

What excites me most isn't simply learning another language or
framework. It's understanding **why** something works, solving
meaningful problems, and watching ideas turn into real applications.

My AI/ML internship introduced me to working with datasets, data
preprocessing, and predictive models. Hackathons taught me teamwork,
creativity, and resilience. Developer communities showed me that
learning becomes more meaningful when it's shared.

Today, I'm continuously improving my skills in software development
while building a strong foundation in AI and Machine Learning.

> **I'm not chasing perfection---I'm building progress, one project at a
> time. 🚀**
